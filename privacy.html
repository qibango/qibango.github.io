棋伴由 Ruilin Yao（个人开发者）发布，并负责本政策所述的个人信息。联系方式：qibango@yahoo.com

棋伴没有账号系统，不需要注册或登录。App 内没有广告、没有数据分析或统计工具，也没有任何第三方追踪代码；App 不会把你的棋谱或使用情况发送给我们。

保存在你设备上的内容

你的对局、研究局面、学习进度、练习题和设置保存在本机的 App 存储空间内。围棋 AI 在你的设备上运行，局面不会发送到服务器。

只有在你主动打开或分享 SGF 棋谱文件时，App 才会读取或写入你选择的那个文件。

棋伴免费提供，没有 App 内购买，也不会要求任何付款信息。

在法律有要求的地区，App 会向 App Store 询问你的年龄段。这一信息只用于遵守该法律（例如在重大更新时请家长同意），只在 App 运行期间保留在内存中，不会保存或发送。

网络连接

App 只在你选用需要下载的网络（均衡、强力或最强）时联网：从 Apple 的 App Store 服务器下载一次，与下载 App 本身相同，不会向我们发送任何内容。App 内部使用一个只在本机（127.0.0.1）可见的通信接口，其他设备无法访问。点击外部链接时会在浏览器中打开。

Who publishes the app

No account, ads or tracking

Qiban has no accounts; you never sign up or sign in. There are no advertisements, no analytics and no third-party tracking code in the app, and the app does not send us your games or how you use it.

What stays on your device

Your games, study positions, progress, drills and settings are stored in the app’s own storage on your device. The Go AI runs on your device; positions are not sent to a server.

Depending on your backup settings, this data may be included in your iCloud or computer backup, which Apple handles under its privacy policy; we do not receive it. Deleting the app removes what it keeps on this device; backups and SGF files you exported stay until you delete them.

The app reads or writes an SGF file only when you choose to open or share that file.

Free of charge

Qiban is free. It has no in-app purchases and never asks for payment details.

Age requirements

Where the law requires it, the app asks the App Store for your age category. It uses the answer only to meet that law — for example to ask a parent to approve a significant update — keeps it in memory while the app runs, and does not store or send it.

Network connections

The app connects to the internet only when you choose a network that is downloaded on demand (Balanced, Strong or Max): it is fetched once from Apple’s App Store servers, like the app itself, and nothing is sent to us. Internally the app uses a channel visible only on the device itself (127.0.0.1), which other devices cannot reach. External links open in your browser.

If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach (an SGF file, for example). Children

The app is suitable for all ages. It does not ask anyone, including children, for personal information.
